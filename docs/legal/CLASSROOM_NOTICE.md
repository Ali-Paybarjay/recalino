# Recalino: information for teachers and classmates

Review copy — 19 October 2026. This copy is prepared for the initial ESOL pilot with up to eight accounts. The owner reports that permission for recording and AI submission will be obtained verbally from the teacher and people present before each recording. This is a limited product test, not a public or commercial college launch. Formal college approval and the final legal release are deferred to the later launch stage.

## What is the pilot?

Ali Paybarjay runs Recalino, a free pilot for up to eight adult learners. A learner can use selected, permitted class material to make a private transcript, study notes and revision cards. You can be in a recording even if you do not have a Recalino account.

## Before anyone records

The learner must follow the college's rules, show this information to the teacher and everyone who may be recorded, and ask before recording. The explanation must include sending selected material to Recalino and its AI services. Permission for a private recording alone does not establish permission for AI processing.

Joining the pilot is optional. You can ask not to have your voice or personal information included. This must not affect your place in the class. If anyone objects, the learner must leave them out or stop the recording. The pilot does not make anonymous voices or reliably identify who is speaking.

Do not record or send personal disclosures about a real person's health, religion, political views, sex life, criminal allegations or similar private matters. General teaching examples about these topics are different from information about an identifiable person. The operator's proposed pilot scope excludes these disclosures; this is not an automatic filter.

## What happens to the material?

The learner keeps the recording on their device. A temporary private server copy is used to make a transcript. That copy expires 24 hours after upload begins and may be removed sooner after transcription; cleanup runs at least once an hour and retries failures. A service failure can delay physical deletion.

Transcripts, selected photos/documents, notes and cards are private to the learner's account until they delete them or the account. AI-generated study material can contain errors or names, so the learner should check it. The learner can download their study notes/cards. Downloaded copies and recordings on other devices cannot be removed by the server.

The app and database use Vercel and Supabase in London. Sign-in uses Google. AI requests use OpenRouter and the model's processing providers. Some processing can happen outside the UK. The app requests zero data retention and denies content collection for routed AI processing; the operator has confirmed OpenRouter content logging/use settings are off. Service metadata and lawful security records may still be retained.

## Who is responsible and how can I ask for help?

The data controller is **Ali Paybarjay**. The pilot contact email is displayed in the Privacy Notice at [recalino.vercel.app](https://recalino.vercel.app) (open “Privacy Notice” on the sign-in page). No Recalino account is needed to contact the operator.

The proposed legal basis for ordinary teacher/classmate data is legitimate interests in helping the learner study their own class, subject to the completed assessment and safeguards. A learner's checkbox does not give consent on your behalf.

You can ask what personal information is held about you, ask for corrections or deletion, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Explain which class/recording you mean; do not send unnecessary sensitive information. The operator may make proportionate checks to find the material and verify the request. Responses must be given without undue delay and normally within one month, subject to legally permitted extensions or clarification. You can complain to the [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## Operator handover before use

Record the college/class, the notice version and delivery date, the permission process and any refusal. Do not collect copies of everyone's identity documents merely to keep a permission log. Do not store private disclosures in the log. This record describes a practical permission/notice process; it is not a blanket Article 9 consent form or a copyright licence.
