# Recalino Terms of Use

Version 0.1 · Draft · 1 October 2026

> **Draft for development only.** This text is not final and has not had a legal review. Parts marked ⟨…⟩ are not finished.

## 1. About these terms

These terms are an agreement between you and {{operator_name}} ("we", "us"). We run Recalino as a small test with an English class. We call this test "the pilot".

Please also read our Privacy Notice. It explains how we use your data.

If something in these terms is not clear, ask the pilot operator: {{operator_email}}

## 2. Who can use Recalino

- You must be 18 or older.
- You need a Google account to sign in.
- Your account is only for you. Don't share it with other people.

## 3. What Recalino does

Recalino helps you study your English class:

- You record your class or add class files, for example photos and PDFs.
- Recalino makes notes and cards from them.
- You review your cards a little every day.

Recalino uses AI services to make transcripts, read photos and documents, write notes, explanations and cards, and make computer voices.

## 4. The pilot is a test

- Recalino is new. Some things may not work well.
- We may change, add or remove features during the pilot.
- Sometimes Recalino may not be available.
- The pilot is free. You don't pay anything.
- The pilot will end one day. Before it ends, we will tell you, so you can download your notes and cards.

## 5. AI can make mistakes

- AI makes your notes, explanations and cards. They can be wrong.
- Recalino shows where each part comes from, for example "From class", "AI explanation" or "Edited by you".
- Some languages are marked "Experimental quality". We have not checked explanations in these languages yet, so we show them next to the English explanation. If you see a mistake, use "Report a problem".
- Check important things with your teacher. Recalino does not replace your teacher. We can't promise exam results.

## 6. Recording your class

When you record a class, your teacher's and classmates' voices are in the recording.

- Before you record, ask your teacher and the people in your class if it is OK.
- Follow your college's rules about recording. If you are not sure, ask your teacher.
- Use recordings only for your own study. Don't share recordings with other people.

It is your job to ask for permission each time you record.

## 7. Class materials you add

- Only add materials that you have the right to use for your private study. Examples: worksheets from your class, photos of the board, your own notes.
- Don't add anything illegal or harmful.
- Don't add private information about other people that you don't need for your study, for example their phone numbers or home addresses.

## 8. Your content

"Your content" means the recordings, files, notes, cards and other things that you add or make in Recalino.

- Adding content to Recalino doesn't make it ours.
- You let us store and use your content only to run Recalino for you. This includes sending it to the services listed in our Privacy Notice, for example the AI services that make transcripts.
- Only you can see your classes, notes and cards. Other users can't see them.
- We don't sell your content. We don't use it for advertising.

## 9. Recordings on your device

- Your recordings are saved on your device, in your browser.
- When you process a class, we send a temporary copy of the audio to our server to make a transcript. We delete that copy automatically.
- If you clear your browser data, or lose or change your phone, recordings that are only on your device can be lost. We can't bring them back. Use "Save a copy to this device" to keep your own copy.
- After processing, your transcript, notes and cards are in your account. You don't need the recording to use them.

## 10. Your allowance

- Processing a class uses your allowance. The pilot operator gives you your allowance.
- Before processing, Recalino shows how much it will use. Nothing is sent until you confirm.
- If you need more, ask the pilot operator.

## 11. Fair and safe use

Don't:

- try to see or change other people's data,
- try to break, overload or attack Recalino,
- try to use more than your allowance, or try to avoid other limits,
- use Recalino to break the law or to harm other people.

## 12. Downloads

- You can download your notes (as a web page) and your cards (for Anki).
- A download is a copy from that moment. It doesn't change when your notes change later.
- We can't delete files that you have already downloaded. Keep them safe.

## 13. Ending your use

- You can stop using Recalino and delete your account at any time.
- We may pause or close your account if you break these terms, or to protect other people or Recalino. We will tell you why, unless the law does not allow it.
- When the pilot ends, we will tell you before we close accounts.

## 14. Our responsibility

- We work hard to keep Recalino working and your data safe. But we can't promise that Recalino will always work, or that AI content will always be right.
- We are not responsible for losses that we could not expect. We are not responsible for recordings that are lost on your device, for example after you clear your browser data.
- Nothing in these terms limits our responsibility where the law does not allow it. Your legal rights as a consumer are not affected.

## 15. Changes to these terms

- If we change these terms, Recalino shows you the new version. You can read it and accept it.
- Until you accept, you can't start new processing. You can still open, download and delete your content.
- If you don't accept, you can delete your account.

## 16. Law

The law of England and Wales applies to these terms. If you live in Scotland or Northern Ireland, you can also go to the courts there.
